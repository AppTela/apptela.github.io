Create dedicated Screens for Work, Creative, or Utilities. Switch instantly. Organize into Categories, bundle into Groups, or use Stacks that expand on hover.

Launch Stacks

How It Works

Step 1: Launch and Scan

AppTela scans your Mac on first launch, building a demo layout with pre-built Stacks to explore immediately.

Step 2: Organize Your Way

Drag apps into Screens, Categories, Groups, or Stacks. Nest Groups for deeper structure. Right-click for actions.

Step 3: Customize Visually

Open the redesigned preferences panel. Adjust themes, shadows, backgrounds with live preview. Apply changes instantly.

Step 4: Launch and Adapt

Single or double-click to launch. Filter by typing. Resize window dynamically. Go fullscreen or keep on top.

FAQ

Yes. It's a visual overlay that never modifies, moves, or removes apps. No data collection.

Absolutely. Supports custom wallpapers, MP4 videos with loop or play-on-change modes, gradients, and more.

How do I switch between setups?

Create multiple Screens and switch instantly. Export/import entire layouts for backup or transfer.

What about performance?

Is this the full activated version?

Yes. AppTela 1.1.0 is fully activated. No purchases or subscriptions needed.
